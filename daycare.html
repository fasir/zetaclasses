FAQ

Before you ask, a few answers

Is Zeta Classes available in my country?
+

We currently run in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. Each country keeps its own school week and its own 4–7 PM local session window — nothing is scheduled on someone else's clock.

What if my child's school uses a different textbook?
+

That's fine — your child brings their own homework and textbook to the session, on camera or as a photo. Our teachers work from whatever page is in front of them, not a fixed lesson plan.

Can my child leave early if homework is done?
+

Yes. Hour 1 is the only compulsory part. If everything's wrapped up, they can log off — the extended hours are there for the days they need more time.

We're relocating to another Gulf country — does the plan move with us?
+

Yes — since the model runs on grade level rather than a fixed school syllabus, moving between the six countries we cover just means switching to the local session window for your new city.
